# MOEFLOW v5.3 · AQUA SKY
清透青空 · 柔软治愈 (Fresh · Gentle · Tactile · Premium)

## 1. Overview
MoeFlow SmartPrint translates high-frequency printing tasks into a healing, delightful, and comforting spatial experience.
Guided by the MoeFlow v5.3 Aqua Sky philosophy, technology feels refreshing, approachable, and effortless rather than intimidating or bureaucratic.

### Visual Keywords
清透、轻盈、柔软、治愈、智能、亲和、高级 (Transparent, Light, Soft, Healing, Intelligent, Approachable, Premium).

## 2. Color Palette & Distribution (60/15/10/5/10)
- 60% Cloud White / Frost White (#F4FBFC, #FFFFFF): Page background, glass cards, generous negative space.
- 15% Aqua Cyan (#20B8B0): Brand primary, hero buttons, key active states.
- 10% Sky Blue (#73C9E8): Secondary actions, file processing, device connection.
- 5% Lavender (#DCCFFF): Creative features, photo printing accents.
- 10% Mint (#B9EBD9), Warm Yellow (#FFF3C4), Peach (#FFD8C8): Status badges, friendly tags, subtle decor.

### Key Hex Tokens
- Primary (Aqua Cyan): `#20B8B0` (Darker: `#168D89`, Light container: `#D5F4F1`)
- Secondary (Sky Blue): `#73C9E8` (Container: `#D7F0FA`, Text on container: `#153D52`)
- Surface Light: `#F4FBFC`
- Surface Container: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface High / Variant: `#EAF5F6`
- Outline: `#C8E3E5`
- Text Primary: `#18343B`
- Text Secondary: `#527078`
- Soft Aqua Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(32, 184, 176, 0.10)`
- Sky Glass Panel: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8);`
- Active Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #20B8B0 0%, #73C9E8 100%)`

## 3. Typography
- Display & Headings: "Plus Jakarta Sans", 700-800, rounded, friendly, modern.
- Body & Labels: "Be Vietnam Pro" / System Chinese fonts, 400-600, clean legibility with relaxed line-height.

## 4. Spacing & Elevation
- 8pt grid rhythm (4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 20px, 24px, 28px).
- Radius: Cards 24px, Small elements 12px-16px, Pills/Capsules 9999px.
- Subtle aqua environmental lighting instead of harsh black dropshadows.
